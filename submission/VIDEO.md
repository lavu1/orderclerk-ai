A narrated walkthrough of real screenshots captured from the running prototype. All demonstration data is fictional. The presentation and narration disclose AI assistance and unverified integrations. No footage of real users or field deployment is implied.

Source: https://github.com/lavu1/orderclerk-ai

Local assets: media/ contains the MP4, original screenshots, storyboard, narration and approximate SRT captions. Rebuild on macOS with Pillow installed: `python3 scripts/render_demo.py submission/media/storyboard.json`. The script uses the system Samantha voice and ffmpeg/ffprobe. Intermediate files remain ignored.
